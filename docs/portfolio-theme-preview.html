Shape & restraint

What the tokens make impossible, and what's still on you.

Do

  • 4px corners everywhere; rounded-full only for dots and avatars
  • One hard shadow per viewport, for the thing that matters
  • At most one orange element on screen
  • Frame every screenshot with .shot

Don't

  • Soft shadows (the token scale is zeroed, so they render as nothing)
  • Borders on every block
  • Fade-in on scroll
  • Scanlines, CRT or glitch effects
